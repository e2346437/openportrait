# Households

A household groups people who share a home or a company, such as partners, parents and children, or colleagues. Each person stays their own contact, with their own history, and each sees the others on their page.

- On a client's page, **Household > Add** and pick someone already in your studio. They join that person's household, which is made (named "Surname household") if it didn't exist.
- **Rename** changes the household's name. **Move** puts them in a different household. **Remove from this household** takes them out; a household with nobody left is deleted.
- A person is in one household at a time.
- With AI access on, `contacts_get` includes the household and the other members.
