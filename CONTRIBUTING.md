# Contributing to OpenPortrait

Thanks for helping. OpenPortrait is licensed under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](LICENSE).

## Before you start

- Read [`docs/SPEC.md`](docs/SPEC.md) for the product scope and architecture, and [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md) for the UI/UX design standards every screen is held to.
- For anything larger than a small fix, open an issue first so we can agree on the approach.

## Contributor agreement

The project maintainer may one day offer a paid edition alongside the open-source one. To keep that possible, every pull request must include this statement in its description:

> I agree that my contribution is licensed to the public under AGPL-3.0, and I grant the OpenPortrait maintainer a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use, modify, and relicense my contribution under other terms. I confirm I have the right to make this contribution.

You keep the copyright to your work. Pull requests without the statement can't be merged.

_Note: this agreement is a plain-language placeholder and should be reviewed by a lawyer before outside contributions are accepted._
